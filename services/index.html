Services

Find the page that matches your water problem

Every page explains the work, the cost factors and what to do while you wait. Twelve of them cover situations that cannot wait, and those pages open with emergency steps. The others cover damage that is serious but usually not urgent in the first hour.

Pipes and plumbing

Supply lines, drains and the pipes under slabs that fail and put water where it does not belong.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamageShutoff steps, cleanup and repair after a supply line fails.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageThaw-time leaks, ice-plug splits and what they wet.
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupTank and fitting leaks that flood utility rooms and closets.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageHidden leaks under concrete slabs and the flooring above.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupContaminated backups through drains and toilets.

Appliances and HVAC

Washers, dishwashers, refrigerators, air handlers and the kitchens they sit in.

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWasher, dishwasher, refrigerator and ice maker leaks.
  • AC Leak Water DamageCondensate overflow into ceilings, closets and hardwood.
  • Kitchen Water DamageCabinets, floors and walls after sink, dishwasher and fridge leaks.

Weather and building envelope

Floods, storms, roofs, basements and crawl spaces where outside water finds a way in.

  • Flood Damage CleanupRunoff, lake and storm flooding, treated as contaminated water.
  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairSagging and stained ceilings from roof leaks or upstairs plumbing.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageStanding water, wet insulation and damp joists under the house.
  • Storm Water DamageWind-driven rain, runoff and tree damage leaks.
  • Basement Water DamageFlooded and seeping lower levels, from sump failures to storms.

Water removal and drying

Pumping and extraction first, then the structural drying that protects wet framing.

  • Water ExtractionPumps and wet vacuums remove standing water, then drying begins.
  • Structural DryingAir movers, dehumidifiers and moisture readings for wet structures.

Floors and walls

Hardwood, carpet, drywall and plaster that need a decision between drying and replacing.

  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageCupping, crowning and buckling, dried in place where possible.
  • Carpet Water DamageExtraction, drying and replacement of wet carpet and pad.
  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageWicking, swelling and stains in drywall and plaster.

Commercial

Shops, offices and multi-unit buildings where downtime matters as much as the water.

  • Commercial Water DamageShops, offices and multi-unit buildings, with phased repairs.

How we scope a job, whatever the service

Every service begins with a conversation and an inspection. We find the source, take moisture readings and describe what we would do and why. Scope and cost factors are explained in writing before repair work begins, and drying and repair are priced as separate parts so you can compare them with an insurance estimate.

If your problem needs a plumber, a roofer or a waterproofing contractor rather than restoration, we say so. Our water extraction and structural drying pages show the two steps almost every job shares.

Choosing a service page

Start with the source. A pipe points to the pipes and plumbing group, a storm points to weather and building envelope, and a wet floor points to floors and walls. If you cannot tell, call and we will help.

This site does not offer mold remediation. We dry wet structures, which is the step that helps prevent mold. If you suspect mold, ask whether your state licenses mold work before hiring anyone.

The emergency line is open every day of the year. Pages that cover urgent situations are marked as emergency pages, and the others are scheduled once the water is under control.
