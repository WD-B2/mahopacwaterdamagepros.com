What a sewage backup looks and smells like

The clues are rarely subtle. Toilets gurgle or overflow, floor drains bubble, and dark water with a strong odor comes up through the lowest fixture in the house. Several drains acting up at once, especially on the lowest level, usually means a main line blockage rather than a single clogged trap.

Backups can come from your own line, from a municipal main or from a failing septic system. Which one it is determines who you call about the blockage. We deal with the cleanup, and a plumber or the sewer district handles the line.

Sewer districts and septic around Mahopac

Parts of the Town of Carmel are served by town sewer districts, and the hazard plan names facilities such as the Putnam Plaza pump station in Sewer District 2 and the Kelly Road pump station. Other homes rely on septic systems, which Putnam County regulates through its Department of Health. Neighbor towns like Putnam Valley record septic systems being affected when lakes and dams overflow.

We cannot tell you which service your address has. Ask the Town of Carmel or your sewer district whether your street is in a district. If you have a septic system, a licensed septic contractor should pump and inspect it after a backup.

How sewage cleanup works

1

Protect and contain

Crews wear gloves, boots and respirators and contain the area so contamination does not spread.

2

Extract the water

Sewage water is pumped out and disposed of properly.

3

Remove porous materials

Carpet, pad, insulation and drywall touched by sewage are removed because they cannot be reliably cleaned.

4

Clean and dry

Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned with disinfectants and then dried before rebuilding.

Safety for you and your household

Do not wade through sewage water or touch it bare-handed. Keep children, pets and anyone with a compromised immune system away. Wash hands thoroughly after any contact. If someone has been exposed and feels unwell, a doctor is the right person to ask.

What to do before we arrive: sewage backup

  • Do not use sinks, toilets or the washing machine, because that sends more water into the drain.
  • Keep children, pets and anyone ill out of the area.
  • Do not touch the water, and wear waterproof boots and gloves if you must enter.
  • Call your sewer authority or a plumber if the blockage may be in the main line.

Reference standard

We use the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard as a reference for how wet structures are evaluated and dried. That is a statement about the standard we follow, not a claim of any certification. Sewage is treated as contaminated water, which affects what can be cleaned and what must be removed.

Insurance and sewage backups

The New York Department of Financial Services notes that water backup and sewage coverage is typically an endorsement to a homeowners policy, and it may not apply if the backup was caused by flood. Read your policy, ask your agent and file as soon as possible. Keep photos and receipts.
