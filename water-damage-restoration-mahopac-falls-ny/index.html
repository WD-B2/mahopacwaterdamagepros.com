Mahopac Falls, the hamlet beside Mahopac

Mahopac Falls is a hamlet in the southwestern part of the Town of Carmel, the same town as Mahopac, with its own post office and the ZIP code 10542. It is centered on Hill Street, which is County Road 32, and Route 6N. The Mahopac Middle and High School campus sits between the two hamlets.

Because it shares a town with Mahopac, the building department, the town flood law and the hazard plan that apply to your house are the same ones that apply to ours. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for every address in the hamlet, including the roads off Hill Street and Route 6N.

Mahopac Falls notes

Hill Street, Route 6N and the Red Mills area

The Putnam County hazard mitigation plan lists Hill Street at Route 6 among the flood-prone spots in the Town of Carmel. That intersection sits on the corridor that connects Mahopac Falls to Mahopac, so a road that floods there affects the route in and out. When a storm closes it, water in nearby cellars and yards is usually part of the same event.

The hamlet is also home to Red Mills, the site of a Revolutionary-era grain mill with a small park on the old mill ground. It is a reminder that this community grew up around moving water. Older properties near streams and former mill races deal with groundwater that newer subdivisions on higher ground do not.

Permits, utilities and neighboring ZIPs

Repairs that involve permits in Mahopac Falls go through the Town of Carmel building department. The town adopted its Flood Damage Prevention Law on January 9, 2013, and ask them how it applies if your house is in a mapped flood zone.

Two nearby places have their own ZIP codes and sit across the line in Westchester County. Shenorock is 10587 and Granite Springs is 10527. Both are in a different county, so permits there go through Westchester town authorities rather than Carmel. The Town of Somers hazard plan lists the Shenorock Dam, a town-owned dam, among its monitored structures. We serve all three ZIP codes.
