The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

What you do before anyone arrives changes the size of the job. Work through these in order and stop as soon as one step is unsafe.

1

Close the main

Shut the main valve where the line enters the house, usually near the meter or in the basement or utility room.

2

Drain the system

Open the lowest faucet so remaining water drains out, and turn off the water heater supply.

3

Kill power where wet

Switch off breakers for flooded rooms if you can reach the panel dry.

4

Document and call

Photograph the pipe and the water, then call us and your insurer.

If you have never located your main, do it this week and tag it. Every minute of running water adds to the repair scope, so a shutoff you can find in seconds is worth a lot.

Why pipes burst in Mahopac and the Putnam County lake belt

Freezing is the textbook cause. Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pressure builds between the ice and a closed faucet until the pipe or fitting gives way. The rupture often happens in a spot that was frozen, and the water only pours out once it thaws.

Age is the other big factor. Older galvanized and copper lines corrode from the inside, and a weakened section can fail without any freeze at all. High water pressure and worn fittings add to the risk. Homes that began as lake cottages sometimes have a patchwork of repairs from different decades, so the weakest point may be a joint that nobody has looked at in years.

What a burst pipe cleanup bill is made of

Repairing the pipe itself is a plumber's job. Our part is the water damage, and these are the factors that move that scope.

How long it ran

A line caught in minutes soaks a room. One that ran overnight reaches walls and the floor below.

What it hit

Finished drywall, hardwood and insulation cost more to dry or replace than concrete and unfinished framing.

How far it spread

Water that passed through a floor opening doubles the number of rooms to dry.

Water category

Clean supply water is the easiest. If it mixed with sewage or sat for days, the scope changes.

Drying equipment

More wet area means more air movers and dehumidifiers running for more days.

Repair choices

Drying and repair are separate lines. You can approve the drying now and decide on repairs after the readings.

We put the scope in writing after the inspection and explain which items are drying and which are repairs.

Burst pipes and winter in the Mahopac area

Putnam County sits north of New York City with colder winters than the five boroughs, and Mahopac's lake homes tend to have long exposed runs in garages, crawl spaces and outside walls. The Town of Somers hazard plan, which covers nearby Baldwin Place, notes that icing is a recurring problem along Route 6. The same cold snaps that ice the road can freeze a poorly protected line.

If you are not sure your plumbing is safe, ask a licensed plumber about insulating exposed runs and where your shutoffs are. Our frozen pipe water damage page covers the thaw side of the problem.

What we remove and what we try to save

  • Wet insulation, which holds water and loses its value once soaked.
  • Drywall that is saturated, swollen or touched by contaminated water.
  • Flooring and pad that cannot be dried back to normal readings.
  • Baseboard and trim that can usually be pulled, dried and reinstalled.
  • Subfloor and framing, which we dry in place almost every time.

The goal is the smallest removal that leaves a dry, stable structure. Your insurer's adjuster will want to see what was removed, so we photograph before and during demolition.

What to do before we arrive: burst pipe

  • Shut off the main water valve, then open a faucet at the lowest level to relieve pressure.
  • Turn off power to wet areas if water is near outlets, and stay out of rooms where water reaches the panel.
  • Move belongings off wet floors and photograph everything before cleanup.
  • Call a plumber for the repair and keep the failed section of pipe.

Reference standard

We use the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard as a reference for how wet structures are evaluated and dried. That is a statement about the standard we follow, not a claim of any certification. Burst supply lines usually involve clean water in the first hours, so timing affects what can be dried in place.
