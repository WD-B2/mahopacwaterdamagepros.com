The lake the hamlet is built around

Lake Mahopac covers about 587 acres, and the Mahopac hamlet wraps around it. The lake has three privately owned islands, Fairy, Petre and Canopus, and it allows motorboats. Homes along and near the shoreline range from long-standing lake cottages to year-round houses, and they share the same Town of Carmel building department and flood law as the rest of Mahopac.

We do not claim a boundary for a Lake Mahopac neighborhood, because the lake is ringed by streets rather than a single area. If you can see the water from your property or you live a few streets back, call and give us the address.

Lake Mahopac notes

The dam, the spillway and Lake Shore Drive in the county plan

The Town of Carmel section of the Putnam County hazard mitigation plan lists the Lake Mahopac dam among the critical facilities in the mapped 1 percent and 0.2 percent annual-chance floodplain. It also says the spillway overflows when debris has built up and the gates have not been opened, and the town reports localized flooding when that happens.

The plan includes a project, tied to a New York State DEC Order on Consent, for an engineering assessment to address dam deficiencies, with an estimate of $100,000.

The plan also lists Lake Shore Drive among the areas where flooding stays problematic because of drainage issues, and it names Lakeshore Drive in Mahopac for outreach to owners of repetitive-loss properties. We are not engineers and we do not assess dams. This is public planning information. The practical point is that water near the lake can come from more than one direction.

Safe first steps at a lakeside house

Work through these only as far as it is safe.

Check for danger

If water is rising or reaches electrical equipment, leave and call emergency services.

Shut off the supply

Close the main water valve only if you can reach it without standing in water.

Avoid dirty water

Lake and drain water can be contaminated, so keep people and pets out of it.

Do not use household vacuums or fans on it

Ordinary equipment is not suitable for standing or contaminated water.

Document

Photograph the source, the water line and damaged items when it is safe to do so.

Insurance records for lakeside losses

Coverage depends on the cause of the loss and the terms of your policy. A homeowners policy and a flood policy cover different events, so ask your agent about both before a storm season, and again when you file.

We can supply photos, moisture readings and a written scope. Those records describe the work. They do not guarantee that an insurer will pay.
