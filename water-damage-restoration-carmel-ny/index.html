Carmel is the seat of Putnam County and part of the same town as Mahopac

Carmel is a hamlet in the Town of Carmel and the county seat of Putnam County. Mahopac is in the same town, so the building department, the flood law and the hazard plan that apply here also apply to our home base. The US 6 and NY 52 corridor links the two hamlets.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Carmel, including the commercial blocks, the older homes near the center and the newer subdivisions on the edge. Flood insurance data in the plan covers the whole town, so the figures in this page describe Carmel and Mahopac together.

Carmel notes

Public buildings in the floodplain

The Putnam County hazard mitigation plan lists critical facilities in the Town of Carmel that sit in mapped floodplains, including the Putnam Hospital Center emergency room, the CoveCare Center, the senior citizen building and the Carmel Historical Society. That list tells you the floodplain is not only a rural lake issue. It includes buildings that people rely on.

For a business or institution in Carmel, flood and water damage planning is a practical question. Our commercial water damage restoration page explains how we run jobs where operations have to resume.

Culverts, creeks and the roads that flood

The plan names Memory Lane, where an undersized cross-culvert is the problem, and Sandy Street among the town's flood-prone spots. It also notes that Peekskill Hollow Road and Route 6 are important routes. During the July 9, 2023 storms, Putnam County declared a state of emergency on July 10 after roads, bridges and culverts were damaged across the county.

Culvert failures matter to homeowners because they redirect water to places that were not designed for it, such as driveways and yards that slope toward a foundation. If you see water running toward your house during a storm, take photos and call us after the rain slows.
