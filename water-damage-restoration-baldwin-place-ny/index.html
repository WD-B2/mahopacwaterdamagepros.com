The problem Baldwin Place has most: water in the basement

Baldwin Place is a community in the Town of Somers in Westchester County, at the junction of US Route 6 and NY Route 118, just over the county line from Mahopac. The Town of Somers hazard mitigation plan states plainly that basement flooding is the most substantial recurring flood problem to structures in the town.

That sentence is the center of this page. Whether the water arrives as storm runoff, a sump failure or a washing machine hose, a Baldwin Place cellar is where it ends up. Emergency dispatch for these calls is available around the clock.

Baldwin Place notes

Drainage trouble spots in the Town of Somers

The Somers plan names specific drainage problems. Annarock Drive has backwater flooding, and the area around Brick Hill Road and Pines Bridge Road near Route 35 has undersized drainage. The Mahopac Avenue bridge over the Muscoot River also floods. These places are in the town that includes Baldwin Place, and they show how local drainage shapes who gets wet when it rains hard.

The plan also says icing is a problem along Route 6, the same road that connects Baldwin Place to Mahopac. Freezing weather there means both slick road conditions and exposed plumbing at risk of splitting.

Flood insurance numbers for the town

As of March 31, 2014, the Town of Somers hazard plan counted 70 National Flood Insurance Program policies and 25 claims. Hurricane Sandy flooded about 10 structures in the town. These numbers are small compared with the number of homes, which tells you most basement water here is not covered by flood insurance. Check whether your homeowners policy has a water backup endorsement.

If water is still spreading in Baldwin Place

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If water is coming through the floor, leave the shutoff for later and call first.

Cut power where wet

Switch off breakers at a dry panel for rooms with standing water.

Raise valuables

Lift boxes and electronics onto shelves or upstairs.

Avoid contaminated water

Water from a floor drain or a flooded yard should not be touched bare-handed.
