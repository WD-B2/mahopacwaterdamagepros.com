Signs of a slab leak you can see and hear

Slab leaks hide, so the clues are indirect. A patch of floor that feels warm underfoot can mean a hot water line is leaking beneath it. A constant hiss when every fixture is off, a water meter that keeps moving and a bill that jumps with no change in use all point the same way.

Damp baseboards, carpet that stays wet along one wall, cracks in grout lines and a musty odor with no obvious source are other clues. None of them proves a slab leak, because a roof leak or a wall leak can look similar. A plumber with leak detection equipment confirms the diagnosis.

How common slab leaks are around Mahopac

We do not have a primary source for how many Mahopac homes sit on slabs, and the hamlet's lakeside and hillside lots mean many houses have basements or crawl spaces instead. Slab leaks matter most in slab-on-grade ranches, additions built on slabs, garages and the concrete floors of basements where pipes run through the slab.

If your house has a slab section, the plumbing beneath it is the part you cannot see. The Town of Carmel building department can tell you about permits for repiping, and a licensed plumber can discuss rerouting overhead rather than breaking the slab.

Slab leak response in order

1

Confirm and isolate

A plumber tests the lines and shuts the section off. We do not begin drying until the water is stopped.

2

Remove standing water

Water that has reached the floor surface is extracted, and wet flooring is evaluated.

3

Dry the slab and flooring

Concrete holds water and dries slowly, so equipment may run longer than on a wood floor.

4

Repair finishes

Flooring, baseboard and wall bases are replaced as needed once readings confirm drying.

What to do before we arrive: slab leak

  • Shut off the main water valve if you hear running water with every fixture off.
  • Look at the water meter with all water off, and note whether it still moves.
  • Move belongings off warm or wet floors and photograph any staining.
  • Call a plumber with leak detection experience to locate the line.

Insurance and slab leaks

Coverage for slab leaks varies widely. Some policies cover the resulting water damage but not the cost of reaching or repairing the pipe. Gradual leaks are often excluded. Ask your agent specifically about slab leaks and keep the plumber's report, because the insurer will want it.
