What to shut off when a water heater leaks

1

Turn off fuel or power

Set a gas unit to pilot or off and switch the breaker off for an electric unit.

2

Close the cold supply

Use the valve on the pipe feeding the tank, or the main if that valve is stuck.

3

Open a hot tap

Open a hot faucet upstairs so water drains from the tank rather than the floor.

If the leak is slow and from a fitting, you may have time to find these. If water is rushing from the tank, call first and then work through them.

Why water heaters fail

Steel tanks corrode from the inside over time. Sediment on the bottom speeds that up and the tank wall eventually gives way, usually at the bottom seam or near a fitting. A small weep at a connection can be a loose union or a failing valve rather than a failed tank.

Where the heater sits decides how much damage a leak causes. A tank in a basement utility area with a drain usually floods the floor. A tank in a second-floor closet or attic can bring down a ceiling, as with the issues covered on our ceiling water damage repair page.

Mahopac notes

Water heaters in older Mahopac homes

Many Mahopac homes have water heaters in basement utility rooms, which are the same lower levels that take groundwater and sump failures. A leak that lands in a finished basement with carpet or stored boxes does more damage than one in a bare utility room with a floor drain.

If your home uses a private well rather than municipal water, the sediment and mineral load can be heavier on the tank. Ask the Town of Carmel water district or your well contractor which applies at your address. We do not assume either.

What to do before we arrive: water heater leak

  • Turn off the cold supply to the tank and switch off its power or gas at the control.
  • Do not touch the tank if you are standing in water near the panel.
  • Photograph the tank label and the damage, and call a plumber for the unit.

Reference standard

What the adjuster will ask about

  • The age of the heater and any recent service.
  • Whether the leak was sudden or slow, since coverage often depends on that.
  • Photos of the unit, the leak point and every wet surface.
  • Receipts for the cleanup and the plumbing repair.

A sudden tank failure is commonly covered, while a leak that has gone on for weeks may be treated as gradual damage. Policy language decides, and your agent can answer questions about yours.
